How to tell in 10 seconds if someone at an event actually wants to keep talking to you.
Don't trust the smile.
Polite people smile right up until they walk away.
What you want to watch is what happens after you ask them something.
If they ask you something back, they're into it.
If they just answer and stop, they're done.
I call it the question back test.
Miss it and you end up being the guy they've been trying to get away from for 5 minutes.
That's only half of it though.
Look at their feet.
If they're pointing at the door, that's where they want to be.
One of these on its own doesn't mean much.
If you see both, you know.
And if they're done, leave first.
They'll remember you as the guy who didn't make it awkward.
