The first thing you say is making the people who could help your business forget you.
You finally go to the event.
You meet someone who could get you your first client.
Hi, nice to meet you. I'm starting an agency.
30 seconds later, they're looking over your shoulder.
You go home thinking you need more confidence.
But the problem was your opener.
You talked about yourself, so they had nothing to grab onto.
Open with them instead.
Notice one thing they chose and say it.
That question you asked the speaker about pricing?
I was wondering the same thing.
If that feels weird to say to a stranger, researchers tested it.
Strangers liked getting compliments a lot more than the people giving them expected.
People forget what you do.
They remember what you noticed about them.
