Make anyone feel comfortable around you in three steps.
And the third one sounds backwards.
One, listen, you're thinking.
I already do, but nobody can see you listening.
They only see proof, but that won't relax someone who's nervous.
Two, don't make them self-conscious.
They already feel watched.
The spotlight effect.
Say you're so quiet and you just turn the spotlight on.
Talk about the music, the food, anything but them.
Now they're relaxed, but relaxed isn't open.
Three, make them open up.
You think you ask more questions.
Actually, questions feel like an interview.
So go first.
Honestly, I almost didn't come tonight.
One small confession and they'll match it.
People don't come back to the most impressive person in the room.
They come back to the one they felt most like themselves around.
