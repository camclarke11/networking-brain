The halo effect means one good trait makes people rate everything about you higher.
And that trait doesn't have to be looks.
In a 1977 study, students watched two videos of the same professor.
Same face, same accent.
In one, he was warm.
In the other, cold.
The warm version?

The cold version?
The exact same things were irritating.
Same guy.
