Being loud is basically asking the room to look at you, and people can feel it.

The loud guy gets real quiet.
I call him the performative leader.
He's not a real leader, but he wants to be one, so he performs.
You're probably thinking, okay, so if I stop being loud, who's going to listen to me?
Well, it was never the volume.
The loudest person in the room is the most confident guy in the room.

So next time you want people to listen, lower your voice instead of raising it.
