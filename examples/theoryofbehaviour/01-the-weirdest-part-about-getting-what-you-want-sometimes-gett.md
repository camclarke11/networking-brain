Sometimes the anticipation feels better than actually getting what you wanted.
You spend months wanting the car, the relationship, the money, the achievement.
And then you finally get it.
And somehow, it doesn't feel as good as you imagined.
But once you finally have it, the uncertainty disappears.
And what once felt extraordinary becomes normal.
You didn't necessarily stop wanting it.
You stopped getting excited by the possibility of it.
