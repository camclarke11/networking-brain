The real reason nobody introduces you happens in your first few seconds.
Whatever they decide about you then, they spend the whole conversation proving right.
That's confirmation bias.
Two guys meet the same person. One says, I'm just figuring stuff out.
The other says, I help coaches get clients from Instagram.
After that, they have pretty much the same conversation.
One walks away as a nice kid. The other is the guy you need to meet.
So, what made the difference? The label.
When people introduce you, they use one line. He's the guy who…
So, give them that line first. Open with one clear thing you do.
But a label on its own won't get you introduced.
Now, their brain is looking for proof. So give it some.
One small thing you've actually done that fits.
Like, I redid my friend's gym page and he got his first leads from it.
No clients yet? Small is fine.
That's the story they'll repeat when they introduce you.
Here's the catch though. You probably don't know what your label is right now.
Ask a friend tonight what they'd say if they had to introduce you.
Then, come back and comment what they said.
