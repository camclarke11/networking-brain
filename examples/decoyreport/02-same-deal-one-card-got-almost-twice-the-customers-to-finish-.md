A car wash handed out 300 loyalty cards. Same deal, two designs. Card A, eight stamps, then a free wash.
Card B, ten stamps, but the first two are already filled in. Nine months later, 19% of card A finished.
Card B, 34%. Almost double. Let's x-ray it. Both cards need exactly eight washes. Nobody got a better deal.
But card B doesn't start at zero. It starts at 20%. And the closer we feel to a goal, the harder we push.
You're not starting a task, you're finishing one. It's everywhere. The coffee card with a free stamp,
the profile that's 60% complete, the progress bar that jumps the second you sign up. So if you sell
anything, never start your customer at zero. Give them the first two stamps. Decoy number two, follow
for the next one.
