In 1984, psychologists April Kresko and Christopher Wetzel ran an experiment in two restaurants in Mississippi.
Waitresses gave back the change in three ways.
No touch, a light touch on the shoulder, or a quick touch on the hand.
Let's x-ray it.
With no touch, the average tip was 12.2%.
A touch on the shoulder, 14.4%.
A touch on the hand, 16.7%.
Half a second of contact.
