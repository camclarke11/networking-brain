Remove one comma, sell for more. $1,499. Written with a comma or without. Same price.
Let's x-ray it. Researchers Keith Coulter, Pilsack Choi, and Kent Monroe found that people judged the price with the comma as bigger.
Why? You read it in your head, and the comma makes it longer. $1,499.
More syllables feel like more money, even when you never say it out loud.
Cents add sellables too. That's why fancy menus just write 28. No dollar sign, no .00.
So if you sell anything, write your price short. Every extra character costs you.
