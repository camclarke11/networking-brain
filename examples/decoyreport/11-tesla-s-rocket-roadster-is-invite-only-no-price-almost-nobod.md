Tesla's flying car isn't for sale. It's there to sell the one that drives. Three versions.
Rocket, the one that flies. Invite only, no price. Thrust, a $250,000 reservation,
limited release. And Performance, the one that just drives. $50,000 to reserve.
Let's x-ray it. Almost nobody will own the Rocket. But it does two jobs. First,
it gets watched. Millions of people saw a car float down from a ramp. No ad could have
bought that attention. Second, it moves the anchor. Next to a car that flies, $50,000 for one that
drives starts to sound reasonable. Small brands can do this too. Add one extreme option almost nobody
buys. A cake for 200 guests. A coaching program by application only. It gets people talking,
and it makes your normal offer look like the smart choice. Your best seller needs a show-off
standing next to it. That's the decoy. Follow for more.
