# The Playbook (working draft)

The product the account sells from its link in bio (`knowledge/01-audience-and-account.md`). It writes itself from the content: **every script adds its move here** (AGENTS.md Step 12), under one of six chapters. When it's time to launch, each chapter gets expanded: the move, why it works (with the sources from the script's fact sheet), a worked example, a fill-in template, and a 10-minute exercise.

Status: **coming soon** (`config.json` → `playbook.status`). Nothing in the videos mentions it yet.

Entry format:
```
### [Move name] ([brand or moment])
**Do this:** [the move, one line]
**Instead of:** [what most people do]
**From:** `scripts/<file>.md` · [pillar] · [status]
**More:** [optional: the extra steps, examples and caveats that don't fit in the video. They live here, not in the caption]
```


## 1. Get clients

### The person standing alone
**Do this:** Walk to the one other person standing alone
**Instead of:** Hover near a group hoping someone lets you in
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-person-standing-alone.md` · The Move · drafted

### The seat next to you
**Do this:** Turn to the person next to you, then go up together
**Instead of:** Join the queue for the speaker
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-seat-next-to-you.md` · The Move · rendered

### Wait for the laugh
**Do this:** Wait for the laugh, step in with one line, then hand the story back
**Instead of:** Drift closer and hover until someone notices you
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-wait-for-the-laugh.md` · The Move · drafted

### The second question
**Do this:** Ask about the answer they just gave
**Instead of:** Jump to the next stock question
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-second-question.md` · The Move · rendered

### The echo
**Do this:** Repeat their last few words back as a question, then stop talking
**Instead of:** Plan your next question while they talk
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-echo.md` · The Move · drafted

### Leave on the high
**Do this:** Leave right after a laugh, with one line
**Instead of:** Stay until it fizzles into small talk
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-leave-on-the-high.md` · The Move · drafted

### The specific follow-up
**Do this:** Her name, the thing she said, and what you did with it
**Instead of:** "Great to meet you last night!"
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-specific-follow-up.md` · The Move · drafted

### The liking gap
**Do this:** Assume they liked you more than you think and text anyway
**Instead of:** Assume it went badly and don't follow up
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-liking-gap.md` · The Move · drafted

### The Thursday test
**Do this:** Send one specific day and time, and read how she answers
**Instead of:** Wait for "sometime" to turn into a plan
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-the-thursday-test.md` · The Move · drafted

### Write the intro yourself
**Do this:** Send a message he can forward without changing a word
**Instead of:** Ask "can you intro me?" and wait
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-write-the-intro.md` · The Move · drafted

### Message three old ones
**Do this:** Message three people you've lost touch with, starting with the ones you only half knew
**Instead of:** Cold DM more strangers
**From:** `scripts/2026-10-20-message-three-old-ones.md` · The Move · drafted
