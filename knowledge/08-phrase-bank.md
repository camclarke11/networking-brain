# 08 — Phrase Bank

Lines grouped by the job they do. The **reference** column shows the shape @theoryofbehaviour used. The **ours** column gives fresh variants to use or remix. Never paste a reference line into a script.

## Re-hooks (mid-video)
| Reference shape | Ours |
|---|---|
| "But most people stop there." | "And this is where almost everyone leaves." |
| "That's only half of it though." | "That gets you in the door. This keeps you there." |
| "But here's where it breaks." | "But do it in the wrong order and it backfires." |
| "But a quick hey won't work." | "But a thank-you text won't cut it." |
| "Here's the catch though." | "There's one thing that ruins all of it." |

## Voicing the objection
| Reference shape | Ours |
|---|---|
| "You're thinking, why would a guy like that help me?" | "Now you're wondering why someone that busy would bother." |
| "I know, you're scared that makes you look clueless." | "It feels needy. It isn't, and here's why." |
| "You're probably thinking, cute pricing trick." | "Sounds too simple to work. That's why nobody does it." |

## Turning to the move
- "So do this instead."
- "Here's what you actually say."
- "Use this line."
- "Give them a job."
- "Ask one question only they can answer."

## Proof lead-ins
- "Researchers tested exactly this."
- "In [year], psychologists [did X]."
- "A study of [who] found [what]."
- "This isn't a guess. [One-line finding]."

## Paired-contrast payoffs (E1 shapes)
- "[A] ends a conversation. [B] starts one."
- "People forget [A]. They remember [B]."
- "Nobody [A]. Everybody [B]."
- "You don't need to be [A]. You need to be [B]."

## Before → after payoffs (E4 shapes)
- "You walked in knowing [nobody]. You walk out [with X]."
- "Last week you were [A]. Now you're [B]."

## Share prompts
- "Send this to the friend who [specific habit]."
- "Send this to whoever's coming with you [tonight / to the event]."
- "Send this to the friend who always [gets stuck / talks too much / never follows up]."

## Words the audience uses
event, mixer, meetup, conference, the room, intro, connector, DM, cold DM, left on read, follow-up, coffee, founder, agency, first client, niche, offer, pitch, LinkedIn, IG bio
