# 01 — The Audience and the Account

## Who we're talking to
**Young guys, roughly 18 to 28, who are starting a business** (an agency, freelancing, coaching, a first product) and know that who they know decides how fast it goes. They are not naturals. They go to an event, stand near the drinks, have one awkward conversation and leave. They DM people they look up to and get left on read.

What they want, in their words:
- "I want to walk into a room where I know nobody and leave with people who'll actually help me."
- "I want the guy I DM to reply."
- "I want people to think of me when someone needs what I do."

What they fear: looking needy, looking clueless, being the guy people try to escape from, being forgotten five minutes later.

**The "this is me" test:** every script must put the viewer somewhere they have been or are about to be. If a 22-year-old with a new agency can't picture using it this week, rewrite the situation.

## What the account is
- **Promise:** psychology-backed moves that get people to like you, remember you and introduce you, even if you're not a natural.
- **Identity the viewer is buying:** "I'm becoming the guy who knows everyone." Every video should feel like one step towards that person.
- **Lead magnet:** a networking playbook (or e-book) that collects the tools. Videos tease it, captions point to it. (The playbook itself is a later piece of work.)
- **Recurring cast:** the videos are animated with a fixed 3D cast (see `12-visual-storyboard.md`), so scripts are written for characters on screen, not a talking head.

## How we beat @theoryofbehaviour
We are in their niche on purpose. We win on:
1. **Volume:** 2 to 3 reels a day, against their roughly one.
2. **Script quality:** every script passes `10-quality-checklist.md`.
3. **A look of our own:** the 3D cast.
4. **Originality:** never reuse their tools, acronyms, examples, studies in the same framing, or lines. Their transcripts in `examples/theoryofbehaviour/` are for structure only.
