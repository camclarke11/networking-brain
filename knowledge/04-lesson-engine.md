# 04 — The Lesson Engine: how the middle works

Elliott's brain builds a **story** out of a chain of problems. This account teaches a **move**, but the same law holds: every line either raises a problem or answers one. A list of tips is a timeline. A chain of "but" and "so" is a video people finish.

---

## 1. The eight beats

Target **130 to 160 words** (about 45 to 55 seconds at 175 words a minute). Hard ceiling 190 words. Percentages are of runtime.

| # | Beat | % | Job | Words | Shape (reference account) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | 0–8% | The situation or the line they say, plus an optional loop. `03-hooks.md` | 8–20 | "When you know nobody at an event, talk to this person first." |
| 2 | **Scene** | 8–20% | Put the viewer and one other person in a real place. Name the other person if it helps. Show the problem happening. | 15–30 | "Find the connector. The guy who knows half the room. Say his name's Daniel." |
| 3 | **The move** | 20–45% | The tool, the person, the exact words. Name it if you can (`06-formats.md` §Naming). Show it in use, in dialogue. | 30–50 | "F is family. You ask, are you from here originally?" |
| 4 | **Objection** | ~45–55% | Say the viewer's doubt out loud, in their words, then answer it in one or two lines. | 10–25 | "You're thinking, why would a guy like that help me? Because introducing people is his favorite thing." |
| 5 | **Proof** *(optional)* | ~50–65% | One study or one historical story, one or two lines, only if it makes the move feel safe to try. | 0–30 | "A 2015 study found people who ask for advice come off as more competent." |
| 6 | **Escalation** | 60–85% | Re-hook ("But most people stop there."), then the second half of the move: the deeper step, the follow-up, the thing that turns it from good to memorable. | 20–40 | "But a quick hey won't work. He'll forget you by the next group. So give him a job." |
| 7 | **Payoff line** | 85–95% | The before → after, or the quotable reframe. Closes the hook's loop. | 8–20 | "You walked in knowing nobody. Now every conversation starts with, oh, you're Daniel's friend." |
| 8 | **Share prompt** | last line | Send it to one specific friend. `05-endings-and-ctas.md` | 6–12 | "Send this to whoever's coming with you." |

**Order is flexible inside the middle** (objection can come before the move; proof can come right after the hook if it is the hook). **Hook first, payoff and share last, always.**

---

## 2. But / so, never "and then" or "also"

- Between every two beats you should be able to write **BUT** or **SO**. If you can only write "and" or "also", you have a list. Cut one item or turn it into a problem.
- Tip lists ("Here are 5 ways…") are banned in the spoken script. Put lists in the caption.
- The reference breakouts use "But most people stop there." / "But a quick hey won't work." exactly where a list would have gone.

## 3. Re-hooks

Short videos still need one or two, at roughly 15 and 30 seconds:
- "But most people stop there."
- "But here's where it breaks."
- "That's only half of it though."
- "And this is the part nobody does."
- "But a [quick hey / thank you / like] won't work."
- "So what do you actually say?"
Write your own in the same shape; don't lift these word for word every time.

## 4. Show, don't explain

The video is animated with characters, so **the move should happen on screen**:
- Not "ask about their goals". Instead: "You ask, where do you want all this to go? Watch his face change."
- Not "be memorable". Instead: the specific line, and the other person's reaction.
Every beat 2 to 7 should be something a 3D character can act out. If a line is pure explanation, it needs an overlay (a tool card, a study card) in the storyboard.

## 5. One move per video

One tool, one person, one line, one test. If the idea has five parts, the video teaches one or two and the caption holds the rest. The FORD video works because the acronym makes four parts feel like one thing.

## 6. The payoff closes the loop

Write the payoff line **before** the hook (`05-endings-and-ctas.md`). Then check:
- Does the last line answer the hook's question?
- Would someone screenshot or repeat it?
- Does it describe the viewer **after** using the move (identity), not just the move?
