# 10 — Quality Checklist (the gate every script passes)

Go through every item. **Hard fails** must be fixed before delivering. For **soft checks**, fix what you can and note any trade-off in "Check before posting".

## HARD FAILS

### Topic
- [ ] Tier-checked against `00-what-performs.md`. Tier 3 ideas are moved into a Tier 1 situation or flagged with a Tier 1 alternative.
- [ ] Set in **one specific situation** a young founder faces (an event, a DM, a coffee, an intro, a follow-up).
- [ ] Not a repeat: checked against `content-log.md` (same move, same tool name or same study framing = repeat).
- [ ] Original: no tool name, acronym, example, character name or line taken from @theoryofbehaviour or any other creator.

### Hook
- [ ] Line 1 names the situation or the line they say, on its own, in under 15 words.
- [ ] The full hook is under 25 words.
- [ ] One meaning only when read once at speed.
- [ ] It has an A-vs-B contrast or a loop that the ending pays off.

### Structure
- [ ] One move only. Extra parts went to the caption.
- [ ] Every beat links to the last with BUT or SO. No "and then", no "also", no spoken lists of tips.
- [ ] The viewer's objection is voiced and answered (unless there truly isn't one; say why in the notes).
- [ ] At least one re-hook before the 60% mark.
- [ ] The move is shown with **exact words** or a concrete action, not described in general terms.

### Ending
- [ ] The payoff line closes the hook's loop.
- [ ] The payoff is under 20 words.
- [ ] At most one spoken CTA, after the payoff. Default: a share prompt naming one specific friend.

### Voice
- [ ] "You" is the viewer, in present tense. "I" appears at most twice.
- [ ] One sentence per line; only spoken words in the script section.
- [ ] No banned words or phrases (`02-voice-and-style.md`). No em-dashes or semicolons in the script.
- [ ] Spelling matches `config.json`.

### Truth and safety
- [ ] Every study claim (year, researchers, what they did, what happened) was checked in a real source this session and is listed in the fact sheet with a link.
- [ ] No invented numbers or percentages.
- [ ] The move is honest and kind: no manipulation, lying or pressure.

### Length
- [ ] 120 to 170 words (hard ceiling 190). Word count and estimated seconds are in the header.

### Storyboard
- [ ] Every script line has exactly one shot, using only cast, sets, actions and overlays from `12-visual-storyboard.md`.
- [ ] Frame one shows the situation in line 1.

## SOFT CHECKS
- [ ] Jagged rhythm: at least two 1 to 3 word lines and at least one line over 12 words.
- [ ] A named character the 3D cast can play.
- [ ] A named tool, if the move is reusable.
- [ ] A paired-contrast line somewhere ("A ends a conversation. B starts one.").
- [ ] The share prompt is specific and a little funny.
- [ ] The caption has a saveable list of exact lines and matches the script.
- [ ] The last line could flow back into the first on a loop.

## The read-aloud test
Read it at speed, out loud, as if to a mate.
- Stumble → shorten.
- Bored → add a BUT or cut.
- Cringe at a line you'd tell them to say → rewrite it until you'd actually say it.

## Self-score (in your reasoning, not the output)
Score 1 to 10 on: **Hook, Situation, Move, Objection, Ending, Voice.** Anything under 8 gets revised before delivering.
